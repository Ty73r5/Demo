    An atlas of the Word

    Where the Logos has walked

    This is a map of Christianity drawn by a believer who holds that all truth is God’s truth. It shows the canon and what was left outside it, the original words behind the English, the Trinity alongside the Trivium, and the older traditions of Egypt, Mesopotamia, Persia, Greece, Rome, India and China. It also gives a fair hearing to the faith’s strongest critics and an honest account of its harms as well as its gifts.

    Open the atlas

    Every claim is marked

    Each statement carries a label: documented fact, scholarly consensus, debated, tradition, interpretive synthesis or confession of faith. You can see what kind of claim you are reading.

    Every verse is quoted exactly

    Tap any red reference to read it in the King James, Young’s Literal, Douay–Rheims, the ASV, the Latin Vulgate, and the Hebrew or Greek with Strong’s numbers.

    Every side is steelmanned

    Canons are argued from each tradition’s best case. Critics such as Ingersoll are given their strongest form before they are answered.
