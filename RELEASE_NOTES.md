An interactive atlas of Christianity with a voice-guided tour.

## Try it

- **Online:** https://raw.githack.com/Ty73r5/Demo/main/index.html
- **Offline:** download `logos-atlas.html` below and double-click it. It is one file with everything inside. An internet connection only improves the fonts.
- **Full site:** download `logos-atlas-site.zip`, unzip it and open `index.html`.

Press **Guided tour** at the top for the narrated walk-through. The voice uses your browser's built-in speech, and Chrome, Edge and Safari have the best voices.

## What's inside

Twelve sections: the time-sliding map; canon and non-canon across six traditions; text and translation with a Verse Lab comparing the original Hebrew and Greek with the KJV, Young's Literal, Douay–Rheims, ASV and Vulgate; a 29-word concordance with Strong's numbers; the Trinity and the Trivium; ancient traditions; the Great Conversation and *Maps of Meaning*; Ingersoll, the critics, Templars, Freemasonry and Rosicrucians; the evidence; fruits and shadows; then and now; and the Way.

Every claim is labelled as fact, consensus, debated, tradition, synthesis or faith. Every quoted verse comes directly from public-domain and openly licensed texts.
